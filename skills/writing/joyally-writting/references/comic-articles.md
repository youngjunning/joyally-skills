# Comic articles: editorial integration

Use this Blog article shape for 漫画文章、漫画拆解, and scene-led technical explainers. Deliver a complete article with verified argument, readable prose, sources, finished comic pages near the relevant explanation, and a distinct cover. The comic itself is made and reviewed by `joyally-image` under its `references/comics.md` workflow.

## Establish the article argument

Read the destination's article and asset conventions. State the reader's question, central explanation, factual sources, version, observed versus illustrative behavior, and what each comic sequence must help the reader understand. Write enough prose to establish these claims independently of the images. A batch of comic pages alone does not fulfill an article request.

Give `joyally-design` the verified facts, intended scene change, approved dialogue and labels, character and project constraints, and final reading surface. It owns the visual direction, storyboard presentation, panel system, page composition, lettering layout, and design acceptance. Give the approved design brief to `joyally-image`, which owns cast identity continuity, pixel art, generation and bounded edits, export, and technical image QA. The article workflow keeps factual and copy acceptance. Do not impose a fixed panel grid before the design phase or compose a title, one illustration, and external chat rows as a comic page.

## Integrate and verify

Place each finished page where it advances the prose. Retain copyable commands, source links, uncertainty, tradeoffs, and result-validation criteria in article text. A comic character may illustrate a mechanism but does not establish a real product's ownership or capabilities; label generated interfaces and example outputs as illustrations.

Check that captions, alt text, claims, and page order match the accepted comic and the article. Inspect the published reading width and a narrow viewport against Joyally Design's accepted composition and Joyally Image's technical review. Keep the destination's editable assets, image format, size budget, upload/readback, and publication rules. Re-exporting a page invalidates the affected review, but an unchanged approved page need not be regenerated for a prose-only edit.

The distinct cover follows [article-cover.md](article-cover.md); an internal comic page does not automatically become the cover. Report article, images, local review, commit, push, deployment, and live readability at their observed states.
